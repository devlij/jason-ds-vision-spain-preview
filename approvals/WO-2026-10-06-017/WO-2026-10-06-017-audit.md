3. Upscale: the padded window is Lanczos-resampled back to the native generation resolution (undoing the master's Lanczos upscale), then Real-ESRGAN x4plus (spandrel, CPU; model sha256 in manifest) is applied, then the exact window is Lanczos-resampled to 1080x1920. **AI upscale is flagged per file** (factor + method in this table and in the manifest).
